Training Partner

AlumnEye

The Expert Eye on Your Career

AlumnEye is France's leading finance career accelerator, specialising in investment banking, private equity, and asset management recruitment preparation. Their alumni network spans the top financial institutions globally.

Through our partnership, ECI members benefit from exclusive access to AlumnEye's Analyst Training Program (ATP) · including financial modelling workshops, pitch construction seminars, and mock interviews with finance professionals.

ATP Training Sessions Financial Modelling Pitch Workshops Career Mentoring

Certification Partner

Reyzo

Finance Certification & Recruitment Platform

Reyzo is a finance certification platform connecting students with recruiters across Asset Management, Equity Research, and Trading/Sales. Tests are supervised by the SFAF (Société Française des Analystes Financiers), ensuring rigour and industry credibility.

Over 30 companies · including Rothschild AM, BNP, and LBP AM · actively use Reyzo to recruit. Recruiters access member profiles directly: no endless job-board scrolling.

Financial Partner

AlphaSights

Knowledge on Demand

AlphaSights connects investors and consultants with expert insight through its knowledge-on-demand platform. Starting this academic year, they are ECI's first dedicated financial partner, directly supporting both the club and the fund.

The partnership runs two on-campus events per year · workshops, networking sessions, or careers events · alongside social media features and member email campaigns. ECI members get a fast track when applying to AlphaSights, invitations to their events, and partner goodies.

International Network

European Alliance of Investment Clubs

1 of 10 clubs selected across 9 European countries

ECI represents France in the European Alliance of Investment Clubs · a network of elite student investment clubs across 9 countries, including HEC Investment Club, Nova Investment Club, Trinity SMF, Amsterdam Investment Club, and clubs from Milan, Brussels, and Lausanne.

As a member club, ECI gains access to online workshops with professionals from leading investment banks, hedge funds, and asset managers · cross-club equity presentations with mixed European teams · and an alliance-wide pitch competition with in-person finals in London.

9 European Countries 10 Member Clubs Online Workshops · IB / HF / AM London Finals Cross-Club Pitches

Official Ambassador Club

NextWise Challenge

Euronext Trading Game · Edition 2026/2027

ECI is an official ambassador club for the NextWise x Euronext Trading Game, a six-month investment challenge bringing together nearly 2,000 students and 300 investment clubs across Europe.

Starting 5 October 2026, ECI members compete solo with a €50,000 virtual portfolio and as club teams with €200,000, presenting their strategy at Pitch Battles hosted at venues including the Carrousel du Louvre and Euronext, with mentoring from professionals at Euronext, the AMF, and CAC 40 companies.
